Welcome

Welcome to Freetown Resort-Gulu

Freetown Resort-Gulu offers a comfortable and convenient place to stay in Gulu for visitors, travellers, workers, students, families and guests visiting the city.

Set on Mama Cave Road in the Pece / Wang OO area, the property is built around a quiet courtyard with private, self-contained rooms.

About

A Convenient Place to Stay in Gulu

Freetown Resort-Gulu is located on Mama Cave Road in the Pece area of Gulu City, next to Indoors Bar in Wang OO — giving guests convenient access around the city.

The property offers comfortable, self-contained accommodation built around guest convenience, whether you're visiting for work, study, or to spend time with family.
